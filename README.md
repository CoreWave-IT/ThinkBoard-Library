# ThinkBoard

### A visual space to think, draw, and learn.

**ThinkBoard by CoreWave IT** is a digital whiteboard experience
designed to help people turn ideas into visuals. Sketch concepts, map
out thoughts, explain topics, and make learning more visual---all in one
workspace.

> **Think. Draw. Understand.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## About ThinkBoard

Learning is not always linear. Sometimes the best way to understand an
idea is to draw it, connect it, rearrange it, and see the bigger
picture.

ThinkBoard is built around that simple idea: a whiteboard-style
workspace where users can express thoughts visually and use drawing as
part of the learning process.

Whether you're explaining a concept, planning an idea, working through a
problem, or making personal study notes, ThinkBoard gives those thoughts
a visual space.

## What it's for

-   **Visual learning** --- make difficult topics easier to explore by
    representing ideas visually.
-   **Freeform thinking** --- sketch, arrange, and connect thoughts on a
    digital board.
-   **Concept explanation** --- use drawings and visual layouts to
    communicate an idea.
-   **Planning and brainstorming** --- map workflows, outlines, mind
    maps, and early concepts.
-   **Study and revision** --- build visual notes that support
    understanding and recall.

## Designed for

-   Students and independent learners
-   Educators and instructors
-   Creators and visual thinkers
-   Teams planning ideas and workflows
-   Anyone who prefers to think with a pen, pointer, or visual canvas
